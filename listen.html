Hello,

You will hear 48 sound environments, one at a time and in a random order. Each lasts about one minute.

Please wear headphones: the recordings are binaural, so they only sound right over headphones. Set a comfortable volume on the first sound and then leave it.

The next screen has two tabs: README, with how the test works, and TEST, where you listen and rate.

Listening test

In each trial of this test, a soundscape is presented. Your task is to rate how “annoying, disturbing or bothersome” you consider each of these soundscapes to be and to consider which sources of noise were significant when rating annoyance. There are no correct or incorrect answers in this test, only personal judgements of subjective impressions.

Each sound is played back using the controls. To advance to the next trial, each sound must be played to completion once. When each sound has completed playing, the playback control will automatically switch off. Sounds can also be stopped prematurely using the controls. Each sound can be played back as many times as desired. Once you have entered your ratings and advanced to the next trial, you will not be able to return to a previous trial to change your ratings.

Please open the ‘TEST’ tab above to start the test. You can review this information at any time during the test by clicking on the ‘README’ tab above.

When evaluating the scenes and providing your responses, please imagine yourself within each presented sound environment, as if you were undertaking a desirable activity within each scene (for example: taking a walk, shopping, sitting in a cafe, sightseeing, etc).

You can listen to the sound more than once, but the sound must be played through at least once to advance.

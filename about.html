About Me

Acoustician, educator and recovering studio engineer, based in Glasgow.

My Story

I’m a Glasgow-based acoustician who came to research through music. I started out recording and mixing local bands at The Audio Lounge in Maryhill, studied music technology and sound for film, then moved into computer science and artificial intelligence, which is how I ended up teaching machines to hear the way people do.

That mix of studio ears and data science shapes how I work: I care about how something actually sounds to the person standing there, not just what a meter says. Alongside my PhD I teach, supervise dissertations and volunteer as a STEM Ambassador in Scotland.

Away from work you’ll find me at the chessboard, the piano or the decks, shooting on film, at the gym, or slowly improving my Spanish.

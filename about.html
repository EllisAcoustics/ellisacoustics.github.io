About Me

Education. Acoustics. Research.Born in Glasgow. Based in Manchester.

My Story

I’m a Scottish novice psychoacoustician who came into research through music. I started out recording, mixing, mastering and producing for local bands in Glasgow. Whilst studying audio formally at undergraduate level, I became interested in A.I. through large language models: how they work, why they work, and why they sometimes don’t. Now I train machines to predict how humans will respond to sound environments of the present and future.

That mix of studio ears and data science shapes how I work: I care about how something actually sounds to the person hearing, in context, not just what a meter says. Once I can gather that information, tools can be built and policy can be informed to form a more positive sounding future for all of us.

Alongside my research, I teach and supervise students at both undergraduate and postgraduate level. My passion for acoustics and Scotland led me to join the Institute of Acoustics Scotland Branch as a Committee Member (co-opt) and Early Careers rep. I also sit on the NASA Unmanned Aircraft System Noise Working Group, and Noise Network Plus A.I. and Digital Working Group.

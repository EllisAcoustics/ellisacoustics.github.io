About Me

Education. Acoustics. Research.Born in Glasgow. Based in Manchester.

My Story

I’m an early-career psychoacoustician from Scotland who came to research through music. I started out recording, mixing, mastering and producing for bands in Glasgow. Whilst studying audio at university, I became fascinated by large language models: how they work, why they work, and why they sometimes don’t. Today, I train machine learning models to predict how people will respond to the sound environments of today and tomorrow.

A background in music production and data science shapes how I work. I care about how something actually sounds to the person hearing it, in context, and not just what a meter says. Once we understand that, we can build better tools, inform policy and forge a better-sounding future for everyone.

Alongside my research, I teach and supervise students up to postgraduate level. My passion for acoustics and Scotland led me to join the Institute of Acoustics Scotland Branch as a Committee Member (co-opt) and Early Careers rep. I am a Member of the NASA Unmanned Aircraft System Noise Working Group, and Noise Network Plus A.I. & Digital Working Group.

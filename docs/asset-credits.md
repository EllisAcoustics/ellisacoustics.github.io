## Typeface

`assets/fonts/sourcerer-*.woff` are Latin subsets of **Sourcerer**, from Nico Verbruggen's
[ebook-fonts](https://github.com/nicoverbruggen/ebook-fonts) collection, a heavier take on
Adobe's Source Serif 4. Both are released under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).
The build step cuts them down to the characters each page uses (`assets/fonts/sub/`).

Credits

Who made what on this site, and the licences it uses.

Type

Headings and body text use your device’s own system font. The serif is Sourcerer from Nico Verbruggen’s ebook-fonts, based on Source Serif by Frank Grießhammer for Adobe, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Drawings and sound

The noise-source drawings, intro animations, ear logo and favicon were drawn for this site. Every sound on the Listen page is synthesised live in your browser.

Logos

Organisation and software logos are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify the organisations I work with and the tools I use. Software icons come from Simple Icons (CC0) and Devicon (MIT). The R logo is © The R Foundation, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Photographs

Photographs of Max Ellis presenting at Forum Acusticum Euronoise 2025, Málaga, Spain, are from Max Ellis’s own collection.

Data

The exposure-response curves on the home page follow Miedema & Oudshoorn (2001) for road, rail and aircraft noise, and Janssen et al. (2011) for wind turbines, as compared in Fredianelli et al. (2019). The wind turbine curve is traced from that comparison.

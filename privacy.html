Privacy

Short version: this site doesn’t track you, doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t collect anything about you.

What I collect

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site, so visiting it doesn’t send your details to Google, Meta or anyone else.

On your device

If you use the light/dark switch, your browser remembers your choice in its local storage under the name theme. It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

The Listen page creates its sounds on your device. It never uses your microphone and sends nothing anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. That is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement; I don’t see or receive those logs.

If you contact me

If you email, phone or message me, I use your details only to reply and keep the conversation. I don’t add you to mailing lists or share your details. Ask me at any time and I’ll delete them.

Links elsewhere

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, ORCID, Google Scholar, publishers and others take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

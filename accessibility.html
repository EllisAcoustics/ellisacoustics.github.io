Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to use this site, whatever device, settings or assistive technology they use.

The aim

The site is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What’s in place

  • Text contrast meets AA in both light and dark mode, checked on every page.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring and a “Skip to content” link on every page.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative drawings are hidden from screen readers.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the opening animations are skipped and the rest stop moving.
  • Windows high-contrast and other forced-colour modes keep every button, box and selected option visible.
  • Pages reflow from 320 pixels wide upwards with no sideways scrolling, and text can be zoomed to 200%.
  • Sound on the Listen page never plays until you press play, and stops when you leave the tab.

Known limitations

  • The Listen page is about sound, so it can’t fully replace hearing it. Its spectrogram is described for screen readers, and the level and pitch are given as text.
  • The opening animation plays on each page. It skips instantly with any key, tap or scroll.
  • The CV PDF is generated from these pages and may not be fully tagged for screen readers. Ask and I’ll send an accessible version.
  • Papers on publishers’ sites follow those publishers’ own accessibility.

How it was tested

Automated checks for contrast, names, headings and layout on every page and width; keyboard-only use; Windows high-contrast emulation; and light and dark mode.
